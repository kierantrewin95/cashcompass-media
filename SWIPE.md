## Patterns to use

1. **Faceless text, music only, looped.** Almost every big outlier is a static or near-static screen with text and a track. A silent reel is the biggest gap in ours.
2. **Numbered listicles** ("10 things...", "7 things I don't...", "15 habits that cut £X a month"). One screen people pause or screenshot to read, which drives saves and rewatches.
3. **Then vs now / income vs reality** comparisons. Cost-of-living relatability beats pure tips.
4. **Surprising UK-specific money facts** ("HMRC will pay...", little-known credits, schemes). Verify every one before posting.
5. **Timely UK news** (Budget 2026 on 28 Oct: First Time Buyer ISA replacing Lifetime ISA, 2.5% deposit scheme). Post within days, verify with official sources.
6. **Opinion ask** for comments ("YAY or NAY?", "which one would you cut?").
