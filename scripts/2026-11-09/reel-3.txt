Marriage could cut your tax by £252.
Every year. It's called Marriage Allowance.
For married couples and civil partners.
One of you earns under £12,570.
The other pays basic rate tax.
The lower earner hands over £1,260 of their allowance.
The higher earner pays up to £252 less tax.
Apply free on gov.uk. Backdate to April 2022.
